      Solve a chemical equilibrium with the Newton-Raphson algorithm.

      The unknowns are the free concentrations of the components. Every dissolved species is beta * prod(c ** coefficient), so the residual to cancel is the difference between the analytical total of each component and the total recomputed from those species. Solid phases enter the active set only when their ion product exceeds their solubility product.

      Parameters

      • model: number[][]

        Stoichiometric coefficients of the dissolved species, one row per component.

      • beta: number[]

        Formation constant of each dissolved species.

      • cTotal: number[]

        Total concentration of each component.

      • c: number[]

        Starting component concentrations.

      • OptionalsolidModel: number[][]

        Stoichiometric coefficients of the solid species.

      • OptionalsolidKsp: number[]

        Solubility (dissociation) product of each solid.

      • OptionalsolidC: number[]

        Starting amounts of the solid species.

      • Optionaloptions: SolverOptions

        Tuning of the algorithm.

      Returns number[] | null

      Concentration of every species, or null if it did not converge.

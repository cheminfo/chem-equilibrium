    Class Helper

    Builds an equilibrium from the bundled database.

    Declare what goes into the solution with addSpecie, and the Helper pulls in every equilibrium reachable from it — so adding CO3-- brings in HCO3-, H2CO3 and water autoprotolysis without you naming them.
